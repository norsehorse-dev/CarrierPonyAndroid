New accounts use OpenPGP v6 keys with post-quantum encryption (ML-KEM-768 + X25519). Accounts you already have keep their keys and still work with everyone. Add a contact by their public key: paste it, open a key file, or pick it from a key app such as OpenKeychain. They get a request to accept or decline. You can import a v6 ML-KEM key as your account. Settings pages have a cleaner layout, and Back goes up one level.
